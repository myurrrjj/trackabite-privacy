Privacy Policy for TrackABite

Last updated: 12 October 2026

TrackABite (“the app”) is an Android application for tracking meals and nutrition. This policy explains what information the app handles, how it is used, who it is shared with, and the choices you have. It applies to the TrackABite app only.

Developer: Mayur Jindal
Contact: mayurjindal905@gmail.com

In short

  • The app has no accounts and no sign-in, and we do not run a server that stores your data.
  • Your meal logs, saved meals and settings are stored only on your device.
  • When you log a meal, the description you type — and any photo you attach — is sent to Google’s Gemini API to be analysed. That is the only time your data leaves your device.
  • We do not show ads, we do not use analytics or tracking, and we do not sell your data.

1. Information the app handles

a) Information you provide

  • Meal descriptions — the text you type describing what you ate.
  • Photos — an image you take with the camera or select from your gallery, if you choose to attach one.

These are used to estimate the nutritional content of the meal.

b) Your Gemini API key

The app requires you to provide your own Google Gemini API key. The key is stored on your device using Android’s encrypted storage (backed by the Android Keystore) and is used only to authenticate requests to Google’s Gemini API. The developer does not receive or store your key.

c) Information stored on your device

  • Meal logs (nutritional values, timestamps, and the text or photo you logged).
  • Saved or favourite meals.
  • Your daily calorie target and other preferences.

None of this is uploaded to the developer.

d) Health data (Health Connect)

If you grant permission, the app reads your “total calories burned” and “active calories burned” from Android Health Connect, so it can show how your intake compares with your activity. This data is read on your device and is not transmitted to the developer.

e) Device permissions

  • Camera — to photograph food you want to log.
  • Internet — to reach Google’s Gemini API.
  • Health Connect read permissions — for the two data types described above.

You can grant or revoke any of these at any time in your device settings.

2. How your information is used

  • To analyse a meal and estimate its calories, macronutrients and micronutrients.
  • To display your logs, saved meals and daily summary.
  • To compare the calories you consume with the calories you burn.

We do not use your information for advertising, profiling, or any purpose beyond operating the app.

3. Sharing with third parties

The only third party involved is Google, through its Gemini API:

  • When you log a meal, the meal description and — if you attach one — the photo are transmitted over an encrypted connection to Google’s Gemini API, operated by Google, in order to generate the nutritional analysis.
  • Google processes this data under its own terms and privacy policy. See Google’s Privacy Policy and the Gemini API terms for details.

We do not share your information with anyone else, and we do not sell it.

4. What the app does not do

  • No user accounts or sign-in.
  • No advertising.
  • No third-party analytics, crash-reporting or tracking SDKs.
  • No selling or renting of personal data.
  • No collection of your contacts, location, or advertising identifiers.

5. Data storage and security

  • All of your data is stored locally on your device.
  • Your Gemini API key is stored using Android’s EncryptedSharedPreferences (AES-256, with the key held in the Android Keystore).
  • Data sent to Google’s Gemini API is transmitted over HTTPS.

6. Retention and deletion

  • We do not retain your data on any server, because we do not operate one.
  • You can delete individual meals within the app at any time.
  • Uninstalling the app removes all data it stored on your device.
  • To delete data that Google may hold from your Gemini API usage, use the controls for your Google account.

7. Your choices

  • Revoke the camera or Health Connect permissions in your device settings.
  • Delete logged meals in the app, clear all app data from your device settings, or uninstall the app.
  • Remove your stored API key from within the app.

8. Children

TrackABite is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has used the app, contact us and we will help you remove any local data (which, in any case, lives only on the device).

9. Health and medical disclaimer

TrackABite provides AI-generated estimates of calories and nutrients. These are approximations for general wellness and informational purposes only. The app is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any condition. It is not a substitute for advice from a qualified healthcare professional. Always consult a professional about your diet, health, or any medical condition.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The “last updated” date above will change when we do. Continued use of the app after an update means you accept the revised policy.
