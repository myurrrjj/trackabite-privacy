Data Deletion for TrackABite

Last updated: 12 October 2026

TrackABite does not use user accounts and does not store your data on any server. Everything the app saves lives only on your own device. That means you are always in full control of deleting it, and there is no copy on our side for us to remove.

In short

  • There is no account and no server — nothing to sign in to, nothing stored in the cloud.
  • You can delete all of the app's data yourself, at any time, using any of the steps below.
  • You can also email us and we will help you delete your data.

How to delete your data

You can remove everything TrackABite has stored on your device in any of these ways:

  1. Delete individual meals in the app. Open TrackABite and delete any meal you no longer want to keep.
  2. Clear the app's data. On your device, go to Settings → Apps → TrackABite → Storage & cache → Clear storage. This erases all meal logs, saved meals, your calorie target and your stored settings.
  3. Uninstall the app. Uninstalling TrackABite removes everything it ever stored on your device.

What is not stored

  • We do not operate any server, and there is no account, so there is no cloud copy of your data.
  • Meal descriptions and any photo you attach are sent to Google’s Gemini API to be analysed. They are not retained by us.

Data processed by Google

When you log a meal, the text you type (and any photo you attach) is sent to Google’s Gemini API for analysis, and is processed under Google’s terms. To manage or delete data associated with your Google account and your Gemini API usage, use Google’s own controls at myaccount.google.com.

Request deletion by email

If you would like help deleting your data, or have any question about it, email mayurjindal905@gmail.com and we will assist you.

This data deletion page applies to the TrackABite Android application. See also our Privacy Policy.
